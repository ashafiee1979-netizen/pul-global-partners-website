## Design approach

- Uses the same approved design tokens and official logo as the Home concept.
- Retains the existing website’s large, direct page heading and solution-oriented framing.
- Improves scanability through four full-width platform bands and consistent service grids.
- Uses the approved PUL position and avoids unsupported cybersecurity or technical-certification claims.
- Ends with a mission-fit section, capability statement action, and requirement-focused call to action.
