# Change Request 003 Home Conversion and Visual Storytelling

- Requested: 2026-09-16
- Requester: Executive Sponsor
- Status: Implemented in local prototype and pending review
- Classification: Moderate

## Requested changes

1. Replace “Explore our Capabilities” with “Discover Our Solutions” and link it to Solutions.
2. Route “Discuss Your Requirements” to a strategy-call scheduling experience.
3. Add a callback form.
4. Increase the prominence of “What we do,” simplify platform cards, apply distinct brand colors, and link each card to its relevant Solutions section.
5. Redesign “How we deliver” as an attractive visual process.
6. Replace the small “Where PUL fits” symbols with stronger AI-generated visuals.

## Implementation

- Added direct Home-to-Solutions and Home-to-Schedule calls to action.
- Added four color-coded platform links with larger icons and titles and removed duplicated service descriptions.
- Added anchored sections for all four platforms on the Solutions page.
- Recast the delivery model as a connected five-stage journey.
- Added three governed, optimized conceptual visuals for PUL's buyer and partner environments.
- Added a dedicated strategy-call request page and a shorter Home callback form.
- Forms are interactive prototype demonstrations pending production CRM, email, and calendar integration.

## Design references

- IDEO design-process guidance informed the iterative visual rhythm.
- Deloitte operating-model transformation guidance informed the connected strategy-to-execution presentation.

## Production dependency

Select and connect the final scheduling provider and form-processing workflow before deployment. Recommended candidates are Microsoft Bookings, Calendly, or Google Appointment Schedules.
