AI adoption becomes useful when it begins with a real workflow: where staff spend time, where information gets stuck, and where quality or response time could improve. Choosing a tool before understanding the task often adds another system without solving the underlying problem.

A practical first pass maps the workflow, identifies sensitive decisions and data, sets human-review points, then pilots one bounded use case. Define who owns the result, what must never be automated, how errors are caught, and what evidence would justify expansion.

## A responsible first pilot

- Choose a specific, repeatable task.
- Set data and approval boundaries.
- Measure quality and staff impact.

Technology should support professional judgment, not obscure accountability. Begin with a clear purpose, a small controlled test, and a review point before expanding use.
