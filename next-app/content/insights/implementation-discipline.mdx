Once a strategy is approved, teams still need to make the work executable. Translate the objective into work packages, owners, dependencies, acceptance criteria, and a reporting rhythm that leaders can use to make timely decisions.

Build vendors, staffing, risk, and field constraints into the operating plan early. Maintain a short issue path and a reliable evidence record. When the plan changes, make the effect on schedule, cost, quality, and outcomes visible before the change becomes the new baseline.

## Keep execution visible

- Name owners and decision rights.
- Track dependencies, risks, and acceptance.
- Close the loop with evidence and learning.

An approved strategy is a starting point. Delivery discipline gives the team a practical way to coordinate work, surface decisions, and respond when conditions change.
